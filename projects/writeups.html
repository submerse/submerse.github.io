Approach

Each writeup follows the same arc: enumeration, the foothold, privilege escalation, and a short "what I'd do differently" reflection. I focus on the reasoning — why I chased a given lead — rather than just pasting commands, because that's the part that actually demonstrates methodology.

How it maps to security work

This is the closest thing to the day-to-day of a penetration tester: structured enumeration, chaining vulnerabilities into a foothold, escalating privilege, and — crucially — writing it up clearly. The reporting matters as much as the exploitation in real engagements.

Tip. Link active boxes only after they retire (HTB rules), and keep these methodology-focused — that's what reads as professional rather than a flag dump.
